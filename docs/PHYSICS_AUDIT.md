# Physics and asset audit (2026-10)

The audit covered the global PhysX settings, all 40 asset annotations, the robot USD, the new kitchen
fixtures, and every failure seen while verifying the SkillNode library in Isaac Sim. The table lists what was
found and what changed. Rebuilt scenes pass `tools/check_scene.py` (3 s: free bodies drift < 1 cm,
articulated parts stay closed, robot holds its pose).

## Settings that were checked and kept

| Item | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Solver | TGS, 16 position iterations, 120 Hz, scene-level CCD | `zeno_skills/physics.py:scene_settings` |
| Contact materials | household 0.6 / 0.5 (average), finger pads 2.0 / 1.8 (max) | `zeno_skills/physics.py:materials` |
| Asset sizes and masses | 40 assets; bounding-box densities 13–650 kg/m³ (containers low because hollow) | `annotations/assets.json` |
| Robot drives | damped position drives, kinematic holonomic base anchor | `zeno_skills/physics.py` |

## Problems found and fixed

| Problem | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Round-container floor made of two overlapping squares covering ~75 % of the inner radius | Small items (2.8 cm cherry tomatoes) fell through the ring next to the wall | Ring of floor tiles out to the wall (`container_collider`) |
| Light, small bodies without CCD | A 12 g tomato tunnelled through the cup floor and fell to z = -40 m | Per-body CCD for objects < 6 cm (`tools/build_tasks.py`) |
| No rolling resistance in PhysX | Round items kept rolling inside a mug for > 5 s | Angular damping 3.0 on the same small bodies |
| `zeno_malo_edu_base.usd` referenced `/visuals/lidar_link`, missing in the physics layer | "Unresolved reference prim path" warning on every load | Empty Xform at `/visuals/lidar_link` (the URDF lidar has no visual mesh) |
| `source_urdf` of 23 assets pointed at a deleted EmbodiedGen `outputs/` folder | Annotation not reproducible from the repo | Regenerated by `tools/prepare_assets.py` (paths now under `assets/asset3d/`) |
| Kitchen fixture colliders without a physics material | PhysX default friction on the stove and island | Household material bound to every fixture collider |
| Corner pinch targeted absolute heights | A notebook resting 8 mm low was pinched above its top | Contact heights relative to the measured bottom (`skills._corner_pinch`) |
| `support_under` used the object's original bottom face | A bottle lying on its side was "on nothing" | Lowest point of the rotated box (`Geometry.support_under`) |
| Pot-lid knob is a low mushroom flaring into the dome | 5 cm pads hit the flare before closing | 2.2 cm knob post (collider + visual, `knob_collider`) as the grasp contact |
| Round objects annotated with one closing axis | Some top pinches had no IK from the side the robot stands on | `round` flag: any diameter is a valid closing direction |
| Long thin objects had one pinch point | A second hand found no independent contact (handover) | `along_offsets` pinch points along the long axis |
| Kitchen corner first placed in the living-room corridor, then too close to the fridge | Blocked the route to the dining room; blocked the fridge door ride | Placement search that keeps key routes and door-ride poses free (`tools/build_kitchen_scene.py`) |
| Stove at counter height (0.86 m) | Lid knob at 1.07 m outside the arm's top-down reach (0.35 m) | Low hob at 0.70 m |
| Refrigerator door limited to 35 deg in the appliance layer | At the "open" goal the free end of the door left ~6 cm beside the cabinet; no can fits through | The kitchen layer overrides the hinge limit to 100 deg (sweep checked free of furniture); the appliance layer and the heat_breakfast scenes keep 35 deg |
| Left gripper's left finger kept its fused convex collider (a wedge into the grasp gap); both left finger joints had torso-lift gains | That finger never closed: every left-hand grasp held the object against one finger only (a handed-over rolling pin fell during the next drive) | Pad box collider on all four finger links and finger gains on the left fingers (`fix_robot`; `patch_left_gripper` applies it at load time to scenes baked before) |
| Wide storage bin: no handles, 1.2 kg | Fingers-down rim pinches on both walls have almost no shared IK; horizontal side grasps put the wrist through the front wall; 1.2 kg exceeded the two-arm lift at the reachable posture | Two 24 cm grip bars on the end walls (collider + visible mesh, 3.5 cm below the rim), 0.5 kg (an empty plastic bin), bin on the dining-table edge; lift with the torso lowered 0.30 and the waist at 0.05 |
| Juice bottle at the island's west end, next to the oatmeal bowl and the rolling pin | Tipped over, it lay 0.13 m from the bowl: no pinch on it cleared the bowl (upright had no grasp) | Bottle moved to free space mid-island (7.65, 1.66); `build_tasks.py` now warns when a `spawn_xy` is rejected instead of sampling silently |
| Tomatoes poured from the 12 cm mug | Loose items leave a near-vertical cup only past ~90 deg of tilt; from a rim pinch the arm reaches ~57 deg over the stove (offline park search and runs), and a sideways tilt swings the cup in the pinch (its centre of mass hangs below the closing axis) so the low lip drifts to the gripper and the tomatoes caught on the hand (video). A shallow prep bowl did not help (still needs ~90 deg) and crowded the island | Known limitation: `pour` is not physically verified; `empty` takes the items out one by one (its first path), pouring stays as its alternative path |
| Base paths ignored furniture within 0.4 m of the start and goal (tight parking) | A short re-park cut straight through the island corner and the robot knocked a rolling pin and a bottle off its edge | Those cells now keep the base centre 0.2 m from every box |
| Floor block placed in the corridor between the stove end and the TV stand | Blocked the base route to the trash-can corner | Moved to open floor at (5.6, 1.05) |
| Every pinch point of long objects treated alike | A spoon was pinched on its bowl flank and slid out | Along-axis pinch points ranked by the section under the pads (± pad span + reach tolerance); the spoon is pinched on its handle only |
| Hold check used the smaller finger | An off-centre rim pinch drives one finger to ~0 while the other holds; reported as a drop | "Closed on nothing" = total finger gap < 4 mm |

## Retired

| Policy | Why |
|---|---|
| `policy_014` floor corner pinch | With parallel jaws the closing axis across a 2.9 cm flat object on the floor runs almost horizontally over its top face: the upper pad never presses the top and the lower pad would have to sit below the floor. It held nothing in 11 Isaac Sim attempts (notebook, books, toy car). Flat objects lying on the floor have no pick path; floor items that fit the gripper (blocks, toys) use `pick/floor_top`. |

## Known, not changed

| Item | Why it stays |
|---|---|
| `breakfast_cup` weighs 30 g for a 15 cm cup (real: ~200 g) | Its mass is baked into `sim/zeno_house.usd` and the existing task scenes; changing the spec alone would make annotation and scene disagree. Light cups are easy to knock while pinching. |
| Containers held by a rim pinch (bowls, trays, mugs by the handle) can pivot during long carries | A one-hand pinch on a thin wall carries little torque; the SkillNode library prefers rim pinches over handles and keeps carries short. |
| Top-down reach falls off quickly above ~1.0 m | Arm geometry: shoulder joints at 0.88–1.12 m. Scenes keep top-down contacts below ~0.95 m. |
| A 17 cm carton cannot be dropped into the storage bin on the bookcase top | Its bottom must clear the 0.85 m rim, which puts the hand near 1.0 m over the middle of a deep shelf; shorter items (soda can) fit. |
