## EmbodiedGen V2 replacement checks

All twelve replacement objects now have V2 textured visual and collision meshes, URDFs, converted USDs, and regenerated grasp annotations. The seed-0 [`recycle_and_store`](../../tasks/recycle_and_store/check/check.json), [`organize_utility_items`](../../tasks/organize_utility_items/check/check.json), and [`kitchen_pot`](../../scenes/kitchen_pot/check/check.json) scenes each pass a three-second stability check: no unstable objects and 0 m robot drift. This establishes placement and contact stability, not task completion.

The V2 pot stands on the kitchen fixture's actual 1.009 m surface. Its side handle faces the robot. The first [V2 handle run](../../runs/kitchen_pot_v2_front_handle_pick/result.json) found no park because the kitchen fixture was represented as one solid AABB from floor to upper cabinets. Splitting that collision proxy at the annotated support levels made collision-aware IK and base planning possible; the regression test also confirms that the base remains blocked by the counter. In the measured [V2 handle attempt](../../runs/kitchen_pot_v2_tucked_handle_pick/result.json), the robot reached the pregrasp and contact sequence, but the torso lagged its target by about 0.24 m, the fingers closed without retaining the handle, and pot lift was 0.000 m. `contract_025` did **not** pass. A fresh V2 [`soda_can` pick](../../runs/v2_recycle_can_pick/result.json) passed `contract_012`, with a retained grasp and 0.0585 m measured lift. A separate [two-Contract V2 rollout](../../runs/v2_recycle_can_into_bin/result.json) passed both `contract_012` and `contract_014`: the can ended inside `wide_storage_bin`, and the bin shifted only 0.0001 m before release. The complete three-object task is still untested with V2 meshes. The scene checks alone imply no physical pick result for the other V2 replacement objects.

A bounded V2 [rim policy attempt](../../runs/kitchen_pot_v2_rim_policy/result.json) checked two candidate rim contacts and found no reachable grasp from a collision-safe base. This is a negative fallback result; it does not establish that all rim azimuths are impossible. The pot therefore has a stable scene and annotated gripper contacts, but no measured successful lift.

The general `contract_025` handle route still passed a fresh physical regression on the original breakfast mug after the policy changes: measured lift 0.0237 m with a retained grasp (`runs/v2_regression_mug_handle/result.json`). This result applies to the mug, not to the V2 pot.
